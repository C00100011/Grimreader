Books bundled for Grimreader's demo mode
=====================================

Both books come from Standard Ebooks (https://standardebooks.org) and are released
under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication): free to use, copy and redistribute.

- frankenstein.epub        Mary Shelley, "Frankenstein, or the Modern Prometheus"
                           https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/mary-shelley/frankenstein
- the-time-machine.epub    H. G. Wells, "The Time Machine"
                           https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/h-g-wells/the-time-machine

Any .epub placed in this folder is added to the demo library.
