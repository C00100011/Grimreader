Grimreader uses it only to run Gemini Nano on your own phone, through Android's AICore. The text of the books you read is not sent to Google or to anyone else by this feature: the model runs on the device. AICore itself downloads and updates the model; see Google's privacy information for Gemini Nano at https://developer.android.com/ai/gemini-nano.
